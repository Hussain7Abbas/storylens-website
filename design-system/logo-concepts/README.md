# Story Lens logo exploration

Three transparent PNG concepts generated with the built-in image-generation tool. These are design proposals, not replacements for the application's current assets. Generated color approximations and small-size legibility should be refined in vector artwork after selection.

1. **Iris Grimoire** — refresh of the supplied magical book. Strongest continuity with the existing identity.
2. **Lensbook** — book and lens fused into one symbol. Recommended for the primary app/extension mark because it communicates the name with a simpler silhouette.
3. **Story Thread** — bookmark and connected identities. Most directly expresses the character/alias/version feature.

Palette requested: iris `#6554C0`, pale iris `#B5A8F5`, ink `#202132`, ivory lavender `#F7F7FB`, soft iris `#EEEBFA`.

## Generation prompts

### 01 — Iris Grimoire

Use case: logo-brand / precise-object-edit. Create ONE refined logo variant for Story Lens, a browser extension that helps readers track characters, aliases and evolving identities in web novels, highlight keywords, and use AI summaries. Input image is the edit target. Preserve its recognizable open magical book silhouette, central spine, slightly raised curved pages, friendly fantasy personality. Rebrand and simplify it for a professional modern app icon. Replace all gold, orange, red, green and brown with the exact Ink & Iris palette: deep iris #6554C0, pale iris #B5A8F5, midnight ink #202132, ivory-lavender #F7F7FB, softer purple #EEEBFA. Use deep iris covers and pale lavender/ivory pages; restrained ink depth. Replace the busy multicolored ornaments with just one bold four-point iris glint on the left and a simple lens-shaped circular glyph on the right. Subtle sculpted depth, smooth clean vector-like edges, broad simple shapes, no texture noise, no detailed page text, no excessive gradients. Symmetric balanced silhouette, generous transparent margins, isolated centered square composition. Must remain recognizable at 32px. No words, no label, no presentation board, no background, no external drop shadow. Genuine transparent alpha background.

Reference: `apps/extension/src/assets/icon.png` in the umbrella repository.

### 02 — Lensbook

Use case: logo-brand. Design ONE excellent distinctive minimal logo symbol for Story Lens, a reading companion browser extension which highlights keywords, tracks characters/aliases/versions across novel chapters and provides AI summaries. Concept name for reasoning only: Lensbook. Fuse a stylized OPEN BOOK and a circular LENS into a single clever cohesive geometric silhouette, with the book's two page halves creating the lower curve of the lens and a small diagonal bookmark at lower right subtly suggesting a magnifier handle. The symbol must feel like an optical reading instrument, not an owl, camera, or generic book with an unrelated magnifying glass pasted on. Strong broad geometry, rounded precise corners, elegant negative space, almost flat vector style, no fine strokes. One small four-point glint inside the lens hints at insight, not a cluster of AI stars. Palette restricted to deep iris #6554C0, pale iris #B5A8F5, ivory lavender #F7F7FB and midnight ink #202132. Deep iris dominant, light lavender small accents. A clean confident app mark readable at 24px, with balanced optical spacing and 16% safe margins. Single centered symbol on genuinely transparent background, square canvas. No text, labels, backdrop tile, shadows, 3D effects, mockup, or presentation sheet. This is a fresh creative direction, polished and contemporary.

### 03 — Story Thread

Use case: logo-brand. Generate ONE original, beautifully resolved logo symbol for Story Lens, a web-novel reading companion that connects character names, aliases, and evolving versions across chapters. Concept: Story Thread. A bold bookmark ribbon with a subtle folded open-book base; within the ribbon a single elegant continuous S-shaped negative-space story thread links three substantial circular nodes, expressing one character's different identities. Make the silhouette distinctive and sophisticated, not a network diagram, DNA helix, circuit board, generic AI sparkle, or plain letter S. Refined flat geometric vector-like design; robust broad shapes and limited details that survive at 24px. Palette restricted to deep iris #6554C0 as primary, pale lavender #B5A8F5, midnight ink #202132, and ivory #F7F7FB. Solid iris ribbon, pale lavender secondary page/fold, luminous ivory negative-space path; modest asymmetry with beautiful balanced proportions. No gradients or 3D, no texture, no tiny thin lines, no text, no labels, no border tile, no mockup. One centered standalone symbol with ample clear margins on genuinely transparent alpha background, square canvas. Professional memorable identity suitable for a browser toolbar, desktop app and website.
